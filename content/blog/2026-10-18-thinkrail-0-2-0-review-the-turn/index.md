Good portfolios make it easy for someone to understand what you build, how you think, and where you can add value.

## Lead with the outcome

For every project, explain the user problem and the result first. Technology belongs in the story, but it should support the outcome rather than replace it.

## Show your contribution clearly

Be specific about what you owned: interface design, data modelling, API work, performance, testing, or delivery. Clear attribution builds trust.

## Keep it current

One strong case study is more useful than a long list of old experiments. Treat your portfolio as a product and improve it as your work evolves.
