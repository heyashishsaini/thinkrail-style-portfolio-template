Before reaching for a framework, I try to understand what success looks like for the person using the product.

## Define the smallest valuable outcome

What can a user do after the first version exists that they cannot do today? That question helps separate essential work from nice-to-have features.

## Make the shape visible

I sketch the key screens, data, and states early. It is much cheaper to correct an unclear flow before it becomes code.

## Build in useful slices

I prefer small end-to-end slices: one flow that works from interface to data to feedback. It gives the team something real to test and improves confidence with every iteration.
